# Feasibility study protocol — designed, not administered

Start with six volunteer consultants selected to expose varied experience and deployment perspectives. Six is a practical first-wave design choice, not a psychometric sample-size requirement or a representative sample. Do not use this wave for pass/fail, rankings, awards or population reliability/fairness claims.

## Preparation

Obtain real participant/data-handling authorization, named reviewer/rater roles and an actual contact route. Review exact item revisions and record verdicts. Keep the authored scoring examples hidden during the first independent calibration attempt. Compare disagreements and clarify anchors; any substantive change needs a new item revision and refreshed packet. This AI review is not substituted for appointed real program reviewers.

Provide the versioned product procedures, excluding the untrusted instruction footer from the candidate reference packet while preserving it in restricted source history. Verify positive/negative scoring cases, exact option/key mapping, practical identity linkage, restricted permissions, keyboard/screen-reader operation and interruption recovery in the existing delivery tools. Test the practical-first section boundary. These are study preconditions, not completed checks.

## Administration

Plan 70 minutes, with documented overrun permitted for feasibility observation and approved accessible equivalents arranged privately. Record section and total times, interruptions and use of references. No outside/generative assistance during the study. Present all practicals before MC access; submit practicals before progressing. Explain the section boundary in advance and verify it does not create an unaddressed access barrier.

Collect an optional brief debrief: unclear wording, interpretation of a selected item, realism, reference/navigation problems, sufficient time and suggested changes. Ask about barriers without soliciting diagnoses. Preserve response evidence and actual handling decisions, not invented summaries.

## Scoring and resources

Each MC is 0/1 under a preannounced omission policy. Each practical has eight independently awarded 0/1 points; absent, incorrect or contradictory required evidence earns zero for that criterion. No negative marking, hidden whole-task fail or overall critical-error fail. Separately flag unsafe recommendations for review.

Both raters independently score every practical: 6 people x 3 tasks x 2 raters x 5 minutes = 180 minutes. Add 30 aggregate rater-minutes of calibration and 30 of adjudication = 240 minutes, or four rater-hours. These are planning estimates, not measured scoring speeds. They are additional to four content-review hours. Facilitation, setup, analysis and any extended re-review require separate capacity; defer administration if that capacity is absent.

Keep each independent criterion score and comment before adjudication. Identify the exact form, item revision/fingerprint, rater and response. Report criterion agreement and types of disagreement descriptively alongside the responses; do not infer an agreement coefficient without appropriate data and analysis. The nine authored examples are not empirical agreement evidence.

## Missing data and analysis

Distinguish intentional omission from interruption, inaccessible delivery and lost records. Preserve raw data and apply only a preannounced authorized rule; never silently impute zero. For complete-case helper analysis, explicitly document exclusions and selection limits. Preserve missingness rather than constructing fictitious full attempts.

Begin with actual participation, form identity, missingness, timing and observed response processes. Inspect item/criterion patterns and rater disagreements. Small-sample numeric summaries are diagnostic, not item-retention thresholds or validity proof. No DIF, IRT, equating, domain pass classifications or predictive claims from this wave.

After revision, plan broader evidence collection appropriate to the intended consequence and population. For any eventual passing standard, define minimally competent performance, obtain qualified independent item/rubric judgments, discuss disagreements, retain rounds and rationale, and separately document the program owner's decision, rounding, compensation, error handling and consequences. Neither 60% nor 80% is adopted from the historical synthetic data.

## Handling and completion

Use pseudonymous study IDs; keep identity mapping separately restricted. Obtain actual consent/use, retention, appeal/contact and accommodation arrangements before collection. Do not invent those organizational facts or enforce an arbitrary deletion date from this template.

The study report should state actual findings, revisions, unresolved issues and the next justified use. This design does not authorize administration, publish a credential or establish that any participant is competent.
