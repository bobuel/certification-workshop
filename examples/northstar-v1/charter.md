# Northstar Implementation Evidence — development charter

Fictional product-2.0 program; program revision 4. Finalized as an illustrative design for Certification Workshop v1, with no live enrollment or credential consequences.

**Intended interpretation:** using supplied procedures, interpret evidence from a bounded deployment, identify unsafe or incomplete conditions, and recommend justified verification, correction and escalation steps.

Audience: partner implementation consultants. Coverage: ingestion/reconciliation, narrow service access, recovery evidence and authorized cutover. Excluded: observed software execution, production incident command, architecture and commercial project management. Course completion and this written assessment are not interchangeable evidence of field competence.

The method uses nine selected-response questions and three written evidence-sheet practicals, existing LMS capabilities and restricted document storage. The source packet includes ingestion 1.0, recovery 1.0, access 2.0 grant/verification rules and the explicitly unchanged token procedure in access 1.0. No new platform, lab or paid service is required.

## Development sequence

1. Confirm the job-task hypotheses with real practitioners using the supplied follow-up instrument. Record dissent, role coverage and evidence gaps.
2. Review the exact current items and rubrics, then calibrate scoring with the authored examples. Preserve actual reviewer decisions separately from this AI design review.
3. Verify accessible, versioned delivery in the existing LMS and evidence-sheet workflow, including practical-first section handling.
4. Recruit six appropriately authorized volunteers across experience levels for feasibility. Schedule a 70-minute working session plus feedback, recording overrun rather than forcing a speed inference.
5. Independently double-score all eighteen practical responses, adjudicate, and inspect clarity, timing and response patterns. Revise and re-review before expanding the study.
6. Plan later validation and standard setting only after a stable interpretation/form and adequate resources exist. No credential launch is inferred from week six.

Resources: four content-review hours plus four scoring/calibration hours, with facilitation, delivery setup and analysis separately assigned. The original four reviewer-hours alone do not cover the full study. If that is a hard total cap, stop at reviewed development materials and defer administration. This is a resource requirement, not an invented funding approval.

Success for this first study means useful evidence about misunderstandings, scoring disagreements and delivery feasibility. It does not mean a particular pass rate, alpha threshold or market-ready credential. Actual authority, consent, handling arrangements and delivery receipts belong to a real implementing organization.
