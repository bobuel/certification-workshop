# Northstar Implementation Evidence — feasibility-study guide

Fictional example for Certification Workshop v1. No enrollment, live study or credential is offered here.

The designed study examines how partner consultants interpret supplied deployment evidence, identify unsafe/incomplete conditions and recommend verification, correction and escalation steps. It does not observe hands-on software execution.

Format: three written evidence-sheet practicals, followed by nine selected-response questions. Submit the practical section before viewing the MC section; you cannot return to it afterward. The proposed working session is 70 minutes; the feasibility protocol records completion time and documented overrun without a speed score. A real organizer must verify accessible navigation and arrange any needed equivalent access before participation.

Use the supplied versioned procedures individually. No outside or generative assistance during the study. A real organizer supplies the clean reference packet and confirms resource rules. Never submit production customer data or credentials.

There are 33 possible raw points: nine one-point questions and three eight-point practicals. Ingestion/access/recovery contribute 12/11/10 points. The study has no passing score, ranking, award or credential consequence. Feedback is for development; there is no operational retake or renewal entitlement.

A real organizer must identify an approved contact for access needs, questions, scoring concerns and participation/data-handling choices, and explain timing, use and retention before collecting anything. No historical product-1.0 result automatically becomes a product-2.0 outcome.
