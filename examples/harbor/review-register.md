# Synthetic review register

All entries in program.json are simulated, with `evidence_kind: synthetic`. No human content expert has approved these items.

The development review checked that the prompts' conditions and answer keys match the fictional source guides, practical anchors sum to their maximum score, and each item maps into its domain/competency. Fingerprints bind acceptances to exact item content. New item revisions or edits without a revision change invalidate those acceptances in the helper.

A real review packet should ask reviewers to assess relevance, accuracy, ambiguity, scoring, language/accessibility and potential bias. Record separate comments and dissent. An accepted item status with no current supporting review must not enter a form. A current unresolved revise/reject verdict blocks it even if a second acceptance exists.
