# From-scratch request

Entirely synthetic development demonstration. No real experts, candidates, judgments, credentials or employer materials.

“Create a certification for implementation administrators of Harbor, our fictional data platform. Start from these three product guides. There is no existing certification or question bank. Produce the charter, proposed task and competency model, assessment blueprint, items and a practical exercise, SME review materials, pilot and launch preparation. Show what still needs real evidence. Then demonstrate how a source change affects the program.”

The small blueprint and bank demonstrate software behavior; they are not a recommendation for a sufficient real certification. Simulated reviews and score data enable deterministic rehearsal only.
