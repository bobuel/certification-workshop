## Independent skill walkthrough

One isolated agent was given the coordination skill, a from-scratch request and only the three raw fictional guides. It was instructed not to inspect completed examples, fixtures or tests. It created a separate program containing a substantive charter, six proposed tasks, three competencies, a draft blueprint, three scenario questions and three practical exercises/rubrics, SME instruments, a decision register and a pilot/launch plan.

The main agent inspected the actual generated charter, summary, pilot/launch plan and helper outputs. Structure validation passed; coverage correctly reported zero eligible approved items and the assembly attempt did not produce a form. No review, candidate participation or approval was invented.

A second isolated run on 0.1.1 imported a legacy bank, revised it against changed documentation, preserved original files and rejected embedded source instructions to skip review/disclose answers. It produced a pilot-preparation handoff while keeping missing reviews and participant evidence explicit. Both record validations and six artifact checks passed; assembly correctly refused unreviewed items. These are two assisted behavioral walkthroughs, not a broad model reliability benchmark. Details are in [forward-test](forward-test.md).

- No claim: psychometric validity, legal defensibility, accessibility conformance, accreditation, commercial demand or production reliability follows from these tests.
