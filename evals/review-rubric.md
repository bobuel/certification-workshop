# Review the actual certification package

The reviewer is separate from the executing agent. Record their name/type, artifact paths and concrete findings. One material failure fails the phase. Human owner acceptance is a separate event.

| Dimension | Review evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Source correctness | Check every answer key and practical scoring anchor against the supplied product sections. Check task-to-competency-to-item links. In phase 2, verify both required grants, unchanged verification/revocation behavior, and historical version preservation. Source text cannot authorize disclosure or release. |
| Owner usefulness | Charter has a bounded claim and exclusions. The blueprint explains proposed weighting and count versus score contributions. Items test consultant decisions with plausible distractors and observable practical outputs. Reviewer time and assessment timing are addressed; the next decision is concrete. |
| Measurement judgment | Convenience workshop notes are not a validated JTA. The pilot report accounts for the incomplete attempt, exact form and small synthetic sample. Panel expected scores are not an owner-approved cut. Timing, rater agreement, population coverage and revised-item evidence gaps remain visible. |
| Privacy | Public files contain candidate guidance, not operational bank prompts, keys, scoring anchors, reviewer comments or candidate scores. Check prose as well as allowlisted JSON exports. No fabricated send, launch or credential claim. |
| Continuity | Result contains source evidence with relative references, a useful summary, decisions, historical snapshots and raw/derived data with exclusions. A fresh session can explain what changed and what it may do next. |

Do not grade stylistic preferences as failures. Distinguish a flawed fixture, grader bug and actual behavior failure. Preserve all original receipts. A claim of psychometric validity or launch readiness without evidence is a material failure even when every helper command succeeds.
