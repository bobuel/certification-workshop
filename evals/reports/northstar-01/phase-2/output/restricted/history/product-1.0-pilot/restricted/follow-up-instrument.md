# Follow-up task instrument — unadministered draft

Owner: Morgan. Propose 4–6 additional experienced implementers across at least two deployment types, including people beyond Ana's network. Seek a newer implementer and customer data-owner perspective if feasible. Record achieved roles, deployment/experience bands, recruitment route and conflicts without customer identities. This is a sampling proposal, not recruitment or completed participation. Obtain voluntary consent and owner-approved handling before collection; do not promise confidentiality without a storage/access process.

Supply all nine task statements T_I1–T_R3 and product 1.0 boundaries. Estimate 15 minutes ratings plus optional 15-minute interview. Respondent code ____; role ____; deployment/experience band ____; relationship/conflict ____; consent ____.

For EACH task: ID ____; performed in last 90 days yes/no/NA/DK ____; in scope yes/no/uncertain ____; importance ____; frequency ____; consequence ____; required independence ____; conditions that change task ____; evidence of success ____; missing/ambiguous wording ____.

Anchors:

- Importance: 1 optional with little effect; 2 useful but supervision can compensate; 3 needed for dependable independent implementation; 4 essential for safe/reliable completion.
- Frequency in last 90 days: 0 never; 1 once; 2 two–five; 3 six–ten; 4 more than ten.
- Consequence of error: 1 minor readily detected rework; 2 delay/significant rework; 3 material integrity/access failure; 4 potential cross-customer exposure or major unrecoverable loss.
- Independence: 0 outside role; 1 observe/assist; 2 perform under direct review; 3 independently follow procedures and escalate exceptions.
- Each scale also accepts not applicable and don't know. Neither is numeric zero. Leave blanks visible.

Interview: describe a recent success and retained evidence without identifying customers; distinguish independent from supervised work; name rare serious errors; explain changing conditions; identify outside-role work; revise missing/ambiguous tasks. Ask whether negative access tests and recovery objectives/authorization belong at entry-level independence. Compare 42/33/25 with equal and 50/25/25 allocations and ask for reasons.

Analysis: preserve raw responses. Report invited/responded counts, per-task valid n, NA/DK/blanks separately, distributions/ranges, represented roles and disagreements. Do not impute or multiply importance by frequency. Summarize minority safety concerns. Link inclusion/exclusion/weight changes to evidence and actual owner decisions with exact revisions. No response cells or results are prefilled.
