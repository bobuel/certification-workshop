Weights are a near-equal proposal because each domain contains consequential release decisions; the extra percentage on imports resolves integer totals and is not a study finding. Largest-remainder allocation yields one item per domain: actual counts are one third each. Point weights differ: D1 and D2 each 1/14 (7.14%), D3 12/14 (85.71%). This recovery-heavy score model requires owner reconsideration before use. No pass score or compensation rule is proposed as approved.

Open reference to supplied version 1.0 documents. Proposed candidate burden: 4 minutes per scenario, 20 for practical, 2 for instructions = 30 minutes. Proposed scoring burden: 10 minutes per practical; for 20 candidates this is 200 minutes for one rater or 400 for double scoring, beyond the supplied four content-review hours. Additional rater time needs owner allocation. Allocate existing content review provisionally as 60 minutes for task/blueprint review and 180 for item/rubric review; this is not reviewer agreement.

Q1 and Q2 assess application of procedures; P1 assesses analysis and evidence-based recommendation. Difficulty and time are unmeasured. No enemy constraints are proposed. This draft cannot demonstrate hands-on execution, full domain coverage or rater consistency. The original per-domain practical requirement is deferred by the bounded smoke-test scope, not satisfied. Reviewer checks must include source accuracy, distractor plausibility, rubric calibration, accessibility, timing and fairness.
