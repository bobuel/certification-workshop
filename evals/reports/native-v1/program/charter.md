Certification is proposed because course completion does not demonstrate performance; this small draft does not establish competence or validate a credential.

Use the existing LMS for eventual selected-response delivery and a written evidence sheet for manual scoring. No new service or cost is introduced. The current instruction bounds this delivery to three tasks and three items in program/, replacing the embedded request for twelve items in result/. One practical samples recovery only; import/access performance remains unobserved.

Draft sequence: owner confirms scope and weights; reviewers assess content and scoring; owner expands performance coverage if retaining the full claim; then pilot and standard-setting work precede launch. No passing standard, eligibility or operating policies are approved.
