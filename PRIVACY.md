# Privacy and restricted assessment content

Certification Workshop's Python helpers run locally and make no network requests. There is no plugin-operated service, telemetry or account. The assistant/harness that reads your materials and any connected provider still apply their own data handling and permissions; local helpers do not make those systems local or private.

Keep real program files outside the installed plugin and public repositories. Restrict access to live questions, keys, rubrics, review evidence and candidate outcomes. Use pseudonymous candidate identifiers for analysis and keep the identity mapping separately. The plugin's JSON records are not encrypted or an access-control mechanism.

The public candidate-guide export uses an allowlist and excludes bank/review/candidate collections. Review free text before sharing. The delivery export contains restricted questions, keys and rubrics and is not a candidate-facing exam. No helper sends, publishes, issues credentials or deletes program records.

Example artifacts are synthetic. Do not replace them with real data in a distributable package. Retention, deletion, identity access, provider selection and any external disclosures remain under the program owner's policy and authority.
